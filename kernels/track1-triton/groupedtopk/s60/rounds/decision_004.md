## Pitfalls and Anti-pattern Consultation

- Consulted `references/anti-patterns.md`; its MLU-only selection and compaction regressions do not transfer as GCU evidence and do not justify a kernel-body or selection-dataflow change. This decision remains host-only.
- Consulted `references/bottleneck-judgment.md`; Round 003 supplies no measured host-time attribution. The selected launcher/context path is a falsifiable host-bound hypothesis and requires same-process Level 2 decomposition plus authoritative interleaved wall timing.
- The `triton_gcu` profile establishes direct Triton launch and `num_warps=1`, but not `fast_libentry`, alternate stream semantics, or a device-duration exporter. The candidate must retain the direct launcher and must not use runtime-launch duration as device time or manufacture a device ratio.
- Current-stream lookup failure is not permission to reuse an unkeyed cache entry. The Host Plan requires the uncached compatibility-preserving path in that case; all output lifetime, metadata invalidation, stream/device, and concurrency properties remain guardrails.

## Rationale and Evidence

Round 003 accepted `triton_grouped_topk_003.py` against the accepted Round 002 reference with an unrounded median reduction from `0.292588 ms` to `0.273673 ms`, or `6.464721724746064%`. Both separately scoped traces retained `1.0` runtime launch per call, while the GCU PrivateUse1 exporter still provided no device-duration events. In the canonical source, `_launch_metadata` acquires `torch.gcu.current_stream(device)` at lines 139-176 and `_output_key` acquires it again at lines 120-137; `forward` calls both paths before packaging the same direct launch at lines 227-255. The source evidence supports a narrowly bounded host launcher/context specialization, not a measured device or host-time claim. Round 004 must compare only against canonical `triton_grouped_topk_003.py` and may be accepted only when correctness, all exact-key/lifecycle/concurrency/device/stream guardrails, targeted host evidence, unchanged one-launch conformance, and at least `5.0%` unrounded paired wall improvement all pass.
